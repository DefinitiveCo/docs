<Update label="August 6, 2026">
  Updates since Flash API launched.

  ## Cross-Chain Market Orders

  Trade across chains in a single order: cross-chain execution between any two supported chains, EVM or Solana, is live for market orders. See [Cross-Chain Orders](/crosschain-orders).

  ## Post-Trade Reporting

  Pull post-trade fill data to power analytics, reporting, and PnL directly in your app. See [Post-Trade Reporting](/post-trade-reporting).

  ## Integrator Dashboard

  Track your integration's activity and fees in one place. Instructions to access are in [Getting Started](/getting-started).

  ## Updating an Existing Order

  Change the limit price of a resting limit, stop-limit, or take-profit-limit order with a single gasless, offchain signature. No cancel and re-place, and the order keeps its `orderId`. See [Updating Orders](/updating-orders).

  ## Asset Search

  Find any tradable asset by symbol, name, or address. One call to `GET /search` returns ranked matches with everything an order needs, plus price, liquidity, and a risk flag. See [Search assets](/api-reference/flash/search).

  ## Cross-Rate Pricing

  Price limit and trigger orders in the pair rate, not just USD. Set `limitCrossPrice` or `crossPrice` to quote the target asset in contra-asset units, alongside the existing notional (USD) basis. See [Pricing basis](/placing-orders#pricing-basis).

  ## More TWAP Control

  Schedule a TWAP to start at a future time with `startTime`, and add a limit price so fills only execute at that price or better. See [TWAP orders](/twap-order).
</Update>
